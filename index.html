About Us

Dr. Priyanka Atreja is an eminent Clinical Psychologist specialized in management of various mental health issues (Depression, Stress, Anxiety, Panic etc) and Psychiatric problems. She holds an experience of 10+ years in providing services in the field of Mental Health. She also conducts Workshops and Seminars in Schools, Corporate and various other institutions which help to promote awareness about mental health and well-being.

She provides an unbiased, non-judgmental, and confidential environment where clients can express themselves freely at Antarman-heart talks. The approach focuses on understanding the root concern and creating practical steps for mental and emotional well-being.
